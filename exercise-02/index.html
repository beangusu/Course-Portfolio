Exercise 02: Field Trip to the Web Design Museum

Exercise Instructions

  1. Visit the Web Design Museum Gallery.
  2. Browse websites from the year 2005 or earlier.
  3. Find one website that you find interesting, charming, or surprising. Take some time and find a website that really interests you.
  4. Copy the link to that website's page in the museum.

Exercise Submission Details

You must select a website from 2005 or earlier and provide a unique, one-to-two-sentence observation about its design or functionality. Submit the URL to the website you chose from the museum and your brief, informal observation statement (1-2 sentences) as a text submission in Canvas.

Submission

The website I chose: https://www.webdesignmuseum.org/gallery/pbs-kids-sprout-in-2005

What stands out to me about PBS Kids Sprout's 2005 website is that the entire experience was built in Flash, with sections like "Play," "TV" and "About Sprout" presented acolorful, hand-illustrated icons instead of a typical text-based navigation menu! Thafully illustrated, non-standard style of navigation is exactly what I picture when think of the "golden age" era of web design, back before everything shifted toward thclean, minimal templates most sites use today.
