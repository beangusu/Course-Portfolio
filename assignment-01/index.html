My Top 5 Movies

  1. Wall-E

    An almost wordless story about a lonely trash-compacting robot that somehow connected with me in a very personal way

  2. Kubo and the Two Strings

    A stop-motion film with some of the most breathtaking stop-motion I've ever seen, with a story that reminded me of my own family

  3. Howl's Moving Castle

    A Studio Ghibli film about a cursed girl, a chaotic wizard, a walking castle, and amazing imaginative world-building

  4. Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse

    One of my favorite movies visually because of how it pushes animation as a storytelling tool, using effects like 3D models mixed with 2D painting

  5. The Secret Life of Walter Mitty

    One of those films that you watch as a kid but don't exactly understand at first, until future rewatches make you realize just how much it clicks with you
